# Unscratched Privacy Policy

Effective date: [effective date]

Unscratched is a free app that helps renters document the condition of a home. This page explains what happens to your information. The short version: it stays on your phone, and we never see it.

## What we collect

Nothing. Unscratched has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking SDKs. We do not collect, receive, or sell any data about you or your inspections.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter (addresses, names, notes, and photos) is stored only on your device. Photos are saved in the app's documents folder. Depending on your settings, iOS may include that folder in your own iCloud device backup. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

## Sharing

The app only shares something when you choose to. If you create a PDF report and share it, you do that yourself through the system share sheet, and you pick where it goes. We do not receive a copy.

## Permissions

- Camera: used only to take inspection photos inside the app. Images are not sent anywhere.
- Notifications: if you set a move-out date and allow notifications, the app schedules reminders locally on your device (7 days before move-out, and 14 and 30 days after). No push notification server is involved and no push token is created.

## Planned optional feature: timestamp receipts

This section describes something that is not in the app today.

We plan to add an optional feature that can send SHA-256 fingerprints (hashes) of your photos to a server so it can return a timestamp receipt. Only the fingerprints would be sent. No photos, addresses, or names would be sent. A fingerprint is a short string of characters that cannot be turned back into the photo.

This policy will be updated before that feature ships.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes its data from your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have an iCloud device backup that included the app, manage or delete that backup in your iCloud settings.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
